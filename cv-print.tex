My research spans thermoacoustic instability, aero-vibroacoustics, structural dynamics, railway noise, and ground-borne vibration, and has resulted in new modelling methodologies, validated digital twins, scientific software, and engineering tools for real-world applications. My research aims to develop interpretable and computationally efficient methods for the modelling, identification, prediction, and design of complex dynamical systems by combining first-principles models with experimental and data-driven approaches. I am particularly interested in multi-physics and multi-scale systems in which interactions, uncertainty, and heterogeneous measurements must be integrated to support prediction, monitoring, optimisation, and engineering decision-making.
